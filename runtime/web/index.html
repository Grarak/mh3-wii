Monster Hunter Tri, recompiled, rendered through WebGPU. Supply your own European disc image (RMHP08, ISO, RVZ or WIA). It is copied once into this site's private browser storage and read from there; nothing is uploaded.

Start opens the launcher, as on the Vita: start the game, go straight to the village, a quest (village, online quests played offline, arena) or a custom quest, make custom quests, and set cheats, widescreen, upscaling, the frame limit, SSAA and the language. No online play.

A gamepad works as a Classic Controller: face buttons by position (east confirms and acts, south evades), triggers as L/R, bumpers as ZL/ZR, Start for the pause menu, Back for the quest menu.

In the launcher: the mouse points at a row and a click selects it, the arrows on a row change its value, the right button goes back, the wheel scrolls and the hints at the bottom are buttons. Keys: arrow keys or W S move, Enter selects, Space goes back, left and right change a value, Q / Shift page through lists, E edits a custom quest (a gamepad: east selects, south goes back, north or west edits).
